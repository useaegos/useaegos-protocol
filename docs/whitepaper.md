# Aegos Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Markets on Solana move faster than any one person can follow. Aegos exists to shorten the distance between what is happening and what a reader understands about it. The mission is narrow on purpose: read open market data carefully, show the numbers next to the ranking, and let people judge for themselves.

Aegos is a reading tool. It does not custody funds, give advice, or ask anyone to trust a score they cannot inspect.

## Principles

1. **Show the working.** Every ranking sits beside the raw figures that produced it.
2. **Prefer open data.** Sources are public and named.
3. **State only what is confirmed.** A fact that has not been announced is not rendered as if it had.
4. **Stay readable.** If a view needs a manual, it is the wrong view.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, live market board, ticker, and the reserved first row for the token.

**Phase 2: Signal engine.** Published scoring rules covering depth, turnover and spend, with the inputs displayed beside each rank.

**Phase 3: Open tooling.** Watchlists, threshold alerts and a public interface for builders.

**Phase 4: Holder voice.** Token holders take part in choosing what gets built next.
